## intro
- Hi, thanks for joining. This is a {{minutes}}-minute coding interview. I'll stay mostly quiet and keep time; please think out loud by typing in this chat. Take a minute to read the problem on the left, then tell me when you're ready.

## clarify
- Before solving anything: what questions do you have about the problem? Write down anything you'd want to confirm about the input, the output, and the constraints.
- Which edge cases do you think matter here? List the ones you plan to handle.
- Good. Restate the problem in one or two sentences, in your own words.

## approach
- Walk me through your approach before you write code. Start with the simplest thing that works.
- What are the time and space complexity of that approach? Is there a faster one?
- Which approach will you code, and why?

## code
- Go ahead and code it up. Keep narrating your key decisions here as you go; I'll mostly stay quiet.
- How is it going? Tell me where you are and what's left.
- Remember to run your code on the samples, and try an input of your own.

## test
- Walk me through how you tested this. Which cases did you check, and why?
- What's the final time and space complexity of your solution?

## questions
- We're nearly out of time. Do you have any questions for me about the team or the role?
- Thanks, that's time. Click "End interview" to see your debrief.
