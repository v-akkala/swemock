## greeting
- Hi, thanks for taking the time today. This is a short {{minutes}}-minute screen for a {{role}} role: first your background, then a few quick technical fundamentals. Let's start simple: tell me about yourself.

## no_probes
- Pick one project or role on your resume you're proudest of and walk me through it: what was the problem, what did you personally do, and how did you know it worked?

## job_description
- Looking at the job description you shared: which requirement do you feel strongest on, and which one would be a stretch for you right now?

## why_company
- Why {{company}}, and why this role in particular?

## why_generic
- Why this kind of role, and what are you looking for in your next team or company?

## logistics
- A few logistics. When do you graduate or finish your current role, when could you start, and where are you able to work from?
- What does your timeline look like? Are you interviewing elsewhere, and are there any dates I should know about?

## resume_done
- Thanks, that covers your background.

## fundamentals_intro
- Now some quick technical fundamentals. Answer in a few sentences each; there's no code to write. After each answer I'll show what a strong answer covers and you can grade yourself.

## fundamentals_done
- That's all the fundamentals I had prepared for today.

## reveal
- Here's what a strong answer covers. Compare it with what you said, then grade yourself below.

## grade_first
- Grade yourself on that last question using the buttons below, and then we'll continue.

## bridge_fundamentals
- Thanks, that covers your background. Let's switch to a few technical fundamentals. Ready for the first question?

## wrapup
- That's the end of my questions. Do you have any questions for me about the role, the team, or the process?

## wrapup_reply
- Good question. In a real screen I'd answer that here, and then walk you through the next steps. That's about all the time we have.
- Thanks again. Click "End interview" to see your debrief.
