## greeting
- Hi, thanks for joining. I'll ask you {{count}} behavioral questions, with a couple of follow-ups on each. For every question, a specific example works best: set the scene briefly, say what you did, and finish with the result. About two to three minutes per answer is right. Type (or speak) your answer here; tell me when you're ready and I'll start.

## probe_own
- What did YOU specifically do, as opposed to the team?

## probe_result
- What was the measurable result?

## closing
- Thanks, that's all the questions I had.

## wrapup
- Before we finish: do you have any questions for me about the team or the role?

## wrapup_reply
- Good question. In a real interview I'd answer that here. That's about all the time we have.
- Thanks again. Click "End interview" to see your debrief.
