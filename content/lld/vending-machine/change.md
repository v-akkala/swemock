The machine now keeps a limited cash float. `VendingMachine(inventory, float_coins=None)` takes an optional dict mapping each coin to how many the machine holds (`None` keeps the unlimited behavior). With a float, change can only be paid out of the float: if the exact change for a sale cannot be made from it, `select` raises `CannotMakeChangeError`, dispenses nothing, and keeps the customer's money inserted. The coins a customer inserted join the float only when a sale completes, and they are not available for making that same sale's change. Change still uses the fewest coins possible. `cancel()` is unaffected, and `float()` returns the current coin counts as a dict.
