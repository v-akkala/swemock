Expenses can now be split by percentage. `add_expense(payer, amount, percentages=...)` accepts a dict mapping each participant to an integer percentage instead of a list of participants; the percentages must add up to exactly 100 (otherwise `LedgerError`), each must be positive, and the payer may or may not be among them. Shares must add up to exactly `amount`: compute each exact share, round every share down to whole cents, then give the leftover cents one at a time to the participants with the largest fractional parts (ties go to the name that sorts first). The equal-split form of `add_expense` keeps working unchanged.
