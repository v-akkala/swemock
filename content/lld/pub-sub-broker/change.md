Subscriptions can now use wildcards. A subscription topic may contain `*`, which matches exactly one dot-separated segment: `orders.*` receives `orders.created` and `orders.paid` but not `orders` or `orders.paid.late`, and `*.created` receives `orders.created` and `users.created`. A message goes to every matching subscription, once each, in the order the subscriptions were made (exact and wildcard subscriptions together). Wildcards are only allowed when subscribing; `publish` still requires a plain topic.
