Different keys can now have different limits. `set_limit(key, capacity, refill_per_second)` gives that key its own bucket settings; a key without an override keeps using the limiter's defaults. Changing a limit refills the bucket up to now first, keeps the tokens the key has (capped at the new capacity), and applies the new rate from then on. `set_limit` validates its arguments exactly like the constructor. (C++: `setLimit`.)
